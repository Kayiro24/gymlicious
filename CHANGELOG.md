# Changelog

All notable changes are listed here. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-15

First public release of **Gymlicious**.

- Daily "Did you work out today?" check-in with backfill and planning.
- Weekly goals with forgiving streaks, XP, levels, 12 badges and short celebrations.
- Workout logging by split, last and max per exercise, personal records.
- Stats with charts, a month heatmap and time-of-day insights.
- Year Dots with an Android live wallpaper and image export.
- Backups as a single JSON file; restore with preview and one-step undo.
- Optional daily and weekly reminders, all on-device.
- Light and YouTube-style dark themes; phones and tablets; up to 200% text.
- No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
