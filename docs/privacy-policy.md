# Gymlicious privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Gymlicious is an open-source workout log that works without an account. This page explains what happens to your information.

## Your data stays on your device
- There are no accounts and no sign-in.
- Your profile, photo, exercises, splits, workout history, daily check-ins, badges and settings are stored only on your phone or tablet.
- We do not run servers that receive your data. We do not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

## Quotes from the internet
If quotes are set to Anime or Mix, the app downloads quotes from the public AnimeChan service. Like any website, AnimeChan can see your device's IP address when the app connects. The app sends no information about you or your workouts. Set quotes to Gym or Off to stop these requests.

## Backups
- **Your backups:** when you export a backup, the app creates a file and you choose where it goes (your device, a cloud drive, a chat). Whoever can open that file can read your data. Keep it somewhere you trust.
- **Android device backup:** Android's built-in backup may include the app's data in your Google account's device backup, if you have it turned on. This is controlled by Android, not by us. Downloaded quotes are excluded.
- **iOS:** app data is part of your iCloud or computer backup if you have those turned on.

## Permissions
- **Camera and photos:** only to take or pick a profile photo. The photo is resized and kept on the device.
- **Save to Photos (iOS):** only to save the Year Dots wallpaper image you created.
- **Notifications:** only if you turn on the weekly backup reminder or the daily "Ready for today?" reminder. Both are scheduled on your device.
- **Internet:** only for downloading quotes.

## Deleting your data
Settings → Backup & restore → Reset all data deletes everything the app stores. Uninstalling the app also removes it from the device.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published here and in the app.

## Contact
Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository, or use the developer contact email shown on the store listing.
